TRYNEARBY — RESTAURANT DATA
Orange County, CA. 50 restaurants.

Format per entry: name, address, cuisine, price, hours, and free-form context.
Price is $ (under $15/person), $$ ($15–30), $$$ ($30–60), $$$$ ($60+).

1. Pollo Verde
1832 W 1st St, Santa Ana, CA 92703
Cuisine: Mexican, grilled chicken
Price: $
Hours: Daily 10am–10pm
Context: Counter service, charcoal-grilled chicken, sides of rice and beans. Very
family-friendly, high chairs available, kids eat free on Tuesdays. Large portions.
Gluten-free options if you skip the tortillas. No alcohol. Parking lot in back.
Gets loud at dinner. Cash and card.

2. Saffron House
4521 Barranca Pkwy, Irvine, CA 92604
Cuisine: North Indian
Price: $$
Hours: Tue–Sun 11:30am–2:30pm, 5pm–9:30pm. Closed Mondays.
Context: Extensive vegetarian menu, roughly half the dishes. Several vegan options
if you ask them to hold the ghee and cream. Lunch buffet on weekdays. Spice level
is adjustable but default is genuinely hot. Good for groups. Street parking is
tight at dinner.

3. Crust & Crumb
17320 Beach Blvd, Huntington Beach, CA 92647
Cuisine: Bakery, sandwiches, coffee
Price: $
Hours: Daily 6:30am–3pm
Context: Sourdough bakery with a small lunch menu. Vegetarian-friendly, a few vegan
pastries. Great for breakfast or a working morning, lots of outlets and wifi.
Not a dinner spot. Line out the door on weekend mornings. Dog-friendly patio.

4. Ironwood Subs
1290 N Euclid St, Anaheim, CA 92801
Cuisine: Deli, sandwiches
Price: $
Hours: Mon–Sat 10am–8pm, Sun 10am–5pm
Context: Old-school Italian deli. Hot and cold subs, house-roasted meats. Limited
vegetarian options, essentially one veggie sub. Fast, cheap, good for takeout and
large catering orders. Eight seats inside. No frills.

5. Kettle Ramen Bar
3033 Bristol St, Costa Mesa, CA 92626
Cuisine: Japanese, ramen
Price: $$
Hours: Daily 11:30am–10pm
Context: Tonkotsu, shoyu, and a legitimately good vegan miso ramen with mushroom
broth. Bar seating, counter-facing. Not great for groups over four. No reservations
and the wait can hit 45 minutes on Friday nights. Gluten-free noodle swap available.

6. Bluebird Cafe
12150 Rosecrans Ave, Norwalk, CA 90650
Cuisine: American breakfast, diner
Price: $
Hours: Daily 6am–2pm
Context: Classic diner. Big breakfasts, bottomless coffee, booths. Family-friendly
and very kid-tolerant. Limited vegetarian options beyond eggs and pancakes. Cash
only. Small parking lot that fills by 9am on weekends.

7. Slice Theory Pizza
12932 Newport Ave, Tustin, CA 92780
Cuisine: Pizza, Italian-American
Price: $$
Hours: Sun–Thu 11am–10pm, Fri–Sat 11am–midnight
Context: New York style by the slice or whole pie. Vegan cheese available. Several
vegetarian pies. Good late-night option. Beer and wine. Counter order, sit wherever.
Gets crowded after 9pm on weekends with a younger crowd.

8. Hearth & Hen
